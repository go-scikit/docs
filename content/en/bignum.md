go-bignum is the home of arbitrary precision in the parity plan: correctly
rounded `big.Float` functions, number theory, and the Go oracle harness.

| Package | Status | Repository |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bigfloat | in development, not yet tagged | [go-bignum/bigfloat](https://github.com/go-bignum/bigfloat) |

## State

From [STATUS.md](https://github.com/go-scikit/roadmap/blob/main/STATUS.md):
the error model, the Ziv loop, roots, constants, and exp and log are done;
trigonometric, hyperbolic and power functions are in progress. No release has
been tagged.
