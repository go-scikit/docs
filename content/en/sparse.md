go-sparse is the home of sparse linear algebra in the parity plan: sparse
containers, orderings, iterative solvers, and later direct solvers and sparse
eigensolvers.

| Package | Status | Repository |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sparse | released v0.1.0 | [go-sparse/sparse](https://github.com/go-sparse/sparse) |

## What is verified

From [STATUS.md](https://github.com/go-scikit/roadmap/blob/main/STATUS.md):
iteration counts equal SciPy's for CG, GMRES, MINRES and LSQR.
